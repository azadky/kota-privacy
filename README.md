# Kota — Privacy Policy

Public privacy policy page for the **Kota** Android app (English + Türkçe).

Live: https://azadky.github.io/kota-privacy/

Kota counts how many short videos you swipe through in the apps you choose
(Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and optionally Snapchat Spotlight and
Facebook Reels) and stops you at the limit you set. The app has no internet
permission; nothing leaves the device.
